# HeronsVote Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** December 01, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Commission on Student Election (COSEL)** of the University of Makati (UMak), acting as the Personal Information Controller (PIC), collects, uses, protects, and handles your Personal Data in compliance with Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (DPA), and its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR).

## II. Personal Data Collected

We collect Personal Data that is adequate, relevant, suitable, necessary, and not excessive in relation to our declared purpose of conducting secure student elections.

We collect the following categories of data when you use HeronsVote:

* **Identity Data:** Full Name, Student Number, College, Program, Year Level, Section, Semester, and Academic Year.
* **Contact Data:** Official UMak Email Address.
* **Verification Documents:** Digital copy of your Certificate of Registration (COR) used for OCR validation.
* **Biometric Verification Data:** We utilize Google ML Kit's Face Detection technology to perform identity verification. This system analyzes specific facial landmarks (such as eye distance, nose position, and face contour) to create a unique, mathematical representation of your face.
    * **Purpose:** This data is used solely to verify that the person attempting to vote matches the registered student account holder, preventing proxy voting and unauthorized access.
    * **Storage:** The facial data is encrypted and stored securely. It is not used for surveillance or shared with third-party recognition databases.
* **Technical/Usage Data:** Device information, IP address, and audit logs of voting activities.

**Note on Mobile Permissions:** The application will request specific permissions strictly necessary for function:
* **Camera:** Required for facial recognition verification.
* **Storage/Files:** Required for uploading your Certificate of Registration (COR).

## III. Purposes and Legal Basis for Processing

The processing of your personal data is for the following declared, specified, and legitimate purposes:

| Purpose of Processing | Description / Scope | Legal Basis (DPA Section) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Identity Verification** | To verify that you are a bona fide student of UMak using your COR and official records. | Consent (Sec. 21(a)) & Legitimate Interest (Election Integrity) |
| **Eligibility Checking** | To filter the ballot so you can only vote for positions relevant to your specific College and Year Level. | Contractual/Service Obligation (Sec. 21(b)) |
| **Secure Voting (Biometrics)** | To prevent proxy voting and ensure the person casting the vote is the registered account holder via facial recognition. | Specific & Informed Consent (Sec. 21(a)) for Sensitive Info |
| **Audit & Security** | To maintain a secure, non-repudiable log of election activities (without revealing your specific vote choices) to detect fraud. | Legitimate Interest & Legal Obligation (Sec. 21(c)) |
| **Communication** | To send in-app notifications regarding election schedules, upcoming deadlines, and official COSEL announcements. | Legitimate Interest (Sec. 21(f)) |

## IV. Collection, Consent, and Data Subject Rights

### A. Data Subject Rights
As a Data Subject, you have the following rights under the DPA:

1.  **Right to be Informed:** You have the right to be informed what data is being collected and why.
2.  **Right to Object:** You have the right to withhold consent to processing (though this will prevent you from using the voting features).
3.  **Right to Access:** You have the right to access a copy of your personal profile data stored in the system via the profile page.
4.  **Right to Rectification:** You have the right to request correction of errors in your student data (e.g., wrong Year Level) by contacting COSEL.
5.  **Right to Erasure:** You have the right to request the removal of your data upon graduation or account deactivation, subject to retention policies.
6.  **Right to Damages:** You have the right to be indemnified for damages due to unauthorized use of your data.

### B. Mechanism for Obtaining Valid Consent
* **Explicit Consent:** You must explicitly agree to this Privacy Policy and the Terms & Conditions by checking a box during the Registration process.
* **Biometric Consent:** You explicitly consent to the scanning and processing of your facial data when you tap "Start Registration" and enable the camera.

### C. Withdrawal of Consent
You may withdraw your consent at any time by requesting account deletion via the Executive/COSEL office. However, withdrawing consent will result in the deactivation of your account and the inability to participate in future digital elections via this platform.

## V. Data Sharing and Recipients

We do not sell your data. Your data is shared only with the following internal parties:
* **COSEL Commissioners & Executives:** For the purpose of validating voter lists, managing election configurations, and resolving protests.
* **System Administrators/Developers:** For technical maintenance, debugging, and security monitoring.
* **University Administration (Authorized Personnel):** Only if required to verify student status against official enrollment records.

We do not share your personal data with external third parties or advertisers.

## VI. Data Retention and Disposal

We adhere to the principle that Personal Data shall not be retained longer than necessary.

**Retention Period:**
* **Account Data:** Retained as long as you are an active, enrolled student at UMak.
* **Election Data (Logs/Ballots):** Retained for the duration of the election period plus the protest period defined in the COSEL guidelines.

**Disposal:** Upon the expiration of the retention period or upon valid request for deletion, digital records will be securely deleted or anonymized to prevent further processing.

## VII. Security Measures and Breach Notification

We implement reasonable and appropriate organizational, physical, and technical security measures, including:
* **Encryption:** Voter data and vote choices are encrypted in the database. Vote choices are stored anonymously and cannot be linked back to your specific identity.
* **Authentication:** Access to the app requires Google OAuth (UMak Email) and biometric verification.
* **Biometric Security:** Facial verification is performed using on-device machine learning models where possible to minimize data exposure. Any facial data transmitted to our servers for synchronization is encrypted both in transit and at rest.
* **Access Control:** Only authorized Executives and Commissioners can access the administrative backend.
* **Breach Notification:** In the event of a personal data breach that poses a real risk of serious harm, we will notify the National Privacy Commission (NPC) and the affected Data Subjects within seventy-two (72) hours of discovery.

## VIII. Updates to this Policy

COSEL may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in election rules or technology. We will notify you of substantial changes via an in-app announcement. Continued use of HeronsVote after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the new terms.
